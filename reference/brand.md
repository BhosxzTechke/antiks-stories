# Brand

> The values below describe the default brand (Antiks Stories). The live values — name, tagline, handle,
> narrator voice, accent colour, host cap/tassel/badge — are in **`data/brand.json`** and override this page.
> Other channels: change brand.json; keep the *structure* of the signature moments, invent your own look.

- **Name:** Antiks Stories (antique + antics). **Tagline:** "Old stories. New antics."
- **Promise:** a story about the Philippines you probably never heard, told in about a minute, true and sourced.
- **Audience:** Filipinos at home and abroad first, plus anyone curious. Explain context in one quick phrase for outsiders; never lecture.
- **Voice:** curious, a little cheeky, never smug about facts. Serious when the story is serious.

## The host (stickman with graduation cap)
- Round white head, thick black lines, stick body, black mortarboard, **gold tassel**, small **8-ray sun** on the cap button (subtle Filipino nod).
- He never narrates (George narrates). He **reacts**: pops in at a corner or takes over the screen in the hook/ending.
- Poses (antiks.js `HOST_POSES`): smug, explain (pointer), thinking, suspicious, shocked, mindblown, blank, sad, happy, wave.
- **Signature gag:** shocked → cap pops up and lands; mindblown → cap flies up spinning and lands (`Antiks.hostReact`). Use the mindblown version at most once per video, on the payoff.
- Tassel always swings — gives life even in still moments.

## Signature moments (every video)
1. Cold open: no logo, text hook on frame 1.
2. Archive reveal: real photo on a paper card, tilted, slams in with flash + shutter sound, source credit under it.
3. Host reaction at the payoff (shocked/mindblown cap gag).
4. Ending: payoff line connects back to the first frame; host does a small wave/smug look; the last caption can be the tagline when it fits — don't force it.

## Colours
- Ink `#111`, gold `#f5c542` (captions keywords, tassel, numbers), paper `#efe6d2` (archive cards), white heads.
- Backgrounds are rich and coloured per story (jungle greens, sea blues, sepia for old documents). Characters stay simple.

## Captions
- Arial Black, white with thick black outline, keywords and numbers in gold, numbers 1.35x bigger. 2–4 words at a time.
