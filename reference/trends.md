# Trends — check, then only use what fits

Trends move fast (audio ~7–10 days, formats ~3–4 weeks), so never rely on a stored list. Each video:

1. **Trend check** (web search, current month): trending short-form *formats/edits* on TikTok, Reels and Shorts, especially animation remixes, POV skits, micro-drama styles, caption styles.
2. **Fit test** — use a trend only if ALL are true:
   - It fits this story's tone (never comedy formats on tragedies).
   - It improves retention at a specific beat (hook, mini-hook or ending).
   - We can do it visually with our own characters, without copying another creator's video.
   - It needs **no copyrighted audio** in the master file.
3. If nothing fits, skip it — original editing beats forced trends.

## Allowed adaptations
- **Host trend cameo:** the stickman performs a 1–2 s version of a trending visual format (timing/pose) in the cold open or ending.
- **Kinetic sound-off captions:** always on.
- **Multi-part series:** for big stories only (see storytelling.md).
- **"Receipts" authenticity:** showing the real document/photo is our version of the low-polish authenticity trend.

## Audio rules
- Master file: never trending/copyrighted songs.
- Optional, platform-native only: adding a trending sound inside the TikTok/Instagram app at very low volume. Not recommended to rely on; never on YouTube; on TikTok keep copyrighted music short (long copyrighted music can make a video ineligible for Creator Rewards).
